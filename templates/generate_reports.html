How reports work

  1. Choose a category (Employee, Payroll, or Compliance).
  2. Select a report from the list.
  3. Refine filters on the right (only filters relevant to that report are shown).
  4. Preview or generate to export as PDF/CSV/XLSX.

Tip: This UI posts filter values to /api/reports/generate. XLSX falls back to CSV.
