How reports work

  1. Choose a category (Employee Data, Deductions, or General Data).
  2. Select a report from the list.
  3. Refine filters on the right (date range, employee, department, deduction types).
  4. Preview or generate to export as PDF/CSV/XLSX.

Tip: This UI posts filter values to /api/reports/generate. Unsupported formats fall back to CSV.
